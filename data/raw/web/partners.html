Partner with Solara

Branch partnership benefits

Operational, marketing and technology support is provided to branch partners. Partners receive a dedicated relationship manager, access to the Solara agent portal, co-funded local marketing, and a full onboarding programme for new staff. Systems, quotation tools and customer documents are supplied and maintained by Solara at no cost to the partner.

What a branch partner provides

  • A staffed location within an agreed territory
  • At least two licensed advisers on site during business hours
  • Adherence to Solara sales conduct and complaint handling standards
  • Monthly reporting through the agent portal

Commission structure

Commission is paid on premiums received, not on premiums written, and is released monthly in arrears. Rates vary by product line and by policy year.

Becoming an accredited agent

Individual agents may be accredited without operating a branch. Accreditation requires a valid licence from the relevant regulator, completion of Solara product training, and a clear regulatory record. Accredited agents receive the same portal access and product support as branch partners, but do not receive co-funded marketing or a relationship manager.

Bancassurance partnerships

Solara works with bank partners in the Philippines to distribute the Bank Partner Plan. Bank staff act as referral partners and are not required to hold an insurance licence, provided they refer rather than advise. All advice and underwriting is carried out by Solara.

Training and support

New partners complete a four day induction covering products, compliance, the agent portal and the quotation tools. Refresher training is provided quarterly, and product update briefings are issued whenever terms change. A partner support line operates during business hours.

How to apply

Partnership enquiries are handled by the regional distribution team. Applications are assessed on location, existing customer base, staffing and regulatory standing. The assessment usually takes three to four weeks.

About Solara Finance Group

Solara Finance Group has served customers across South East Asia since 2009. The group operates health insurance, life insurance and consumer financing businesses through a network of 240 branches and more than 3,000 accredited agents. Solara is regulated in each market in which it operates.
