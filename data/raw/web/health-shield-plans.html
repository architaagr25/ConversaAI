Solara Health Shield

Health Shield is our hospitalisation and medical cover for individuals and families. Three plan levels are available. All plans include in-patient treatment, day surgery and emergency care at accredited hospitals.

Who can apply

Health Shield is available to applicants aged 18 to 60 at entry. Cover may be renewed annually up to age 70. Dependants aged 6 months to 21 years may be added to a principal member's plan. Applicants must be residents of the Philippines and must not be receiving in-patient treatment at the time of application.

Waiting periods

A waiting period of 30 days applies to all illnesses from the start of cover. Accidents are covered from day one. Pre-existing conditions are covered after 24 months of continuous membership, provided the condition was declared at application.

Specific procedures carry their own waiting periods: cataract surgery, hernia repair and removal of tonsils are covered after 12 months.

What is not covered

  • Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures
  • Dental treatment, except reconstructive work following an accident
  • Fertility treatment and pregnancy, unless the maternity rider is attached
  • Self-inflicted injury and injury sustained while committing an offence
  • Treatment outside the Philippines, unless the international rider is attached
  • Conditions arising from a declared epidemic where a government scheme provides cover

Monthly premium

Premiums start at PHP 1,180 per month for Essential cover at age 25. Your contribution depends on your age at entry, the plan chosen and any riders attached. A full rate table is available from any branch.

Riders

Optional add-ons may be attached at application or at renewal:

  • Maternity — normal delivery and caesarean, after a 10 month waiting period
  • International treatment — planned treatment in Singapore, Thailand or Malaysia
  • Critical illness — lump sum on diagnosis of a listed condition
  • Daily hospital cash — a fixed daily amount during any covered admission

How to apply

Applications may be made online, at any Solara branch, or through an accredited agent. Applicants aged 51 and above, and any applicant selecting Max cover, must complete a medical questionnaire. A medical examination is required only where the questionnaire indicates it. Cover begins on the first day of the month following approval and receipt of the first premium.
