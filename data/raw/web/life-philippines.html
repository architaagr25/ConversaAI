Solara Life Philippines

Life cover for Filipino families, available directly and through our bank partners. Protect what your family depends on, whether that is your income, a mortgage, or your children's education.

Our plans

Solara Term Life

Pure protection for a fixed period of 10, 15 or 20 years. The lowest monthly contribution for the highest sum assured. Coverage from PHP 500,000 to PHP 10,000,000. No cash value; if the term ends and no claim has been made, cover simply stops.

Solara Whole Life

Cover for life, with a cash value that builds over time. Premium payment terms of 10, 15 or 20 years, after which the policy is fully paid and no further bayad is required. Cash value may be borrowed against after the third policy year.

Solara Educational Plan

Guaranteed payouts when your child reaches college age, with life cover on the paying parent throughout. If the parent dies before the plan matures, remaining premiums are waived and the payouts still happen as scheduled.

Solara Bank Partner Plan

Available only through our bancassurance partners. Simplified underwriting, a shorter application, and payment collected directly from the linked deposit account. Sum assured up to PHP 3,000,000 without medical examination for applicants under 46.

Paying your premium

You may pay monthly, quarterly, semi-annually or annually. Paying annually costs less overall. The monthly due may be collected by automatic debit, over the counter at any branch, through our bank partners, or through accredited payment centres.

Kung hindi po nabayaran ang hulog sa takdang araw, may grace period po na 31 days. Habang nasa grace period, tuloy pa rin ang coverage niyo.

Lapse and reinstatement

If the contribution is not received by the end of the grace period, the policy lapses and cover stops. A lapsed policy that has built cash value may continue automatically under the automatic premium loan provision, where the cash value is used to pay the overdue amount.

Reinstatement is possible within 3 years of the lapse date. You will need to pay all arrears with interest and provide evidence of insurability. Reinstatement is not automatic and is subject to underwriting.

Beneficiaries

You may name any person as beneficiary. A beneficiary may be revocable, meaning you can change them at any time, or irrevocable, meaning their written consent is needed for any change. If no beneficiary is named, or all named beneficiaries have died, proceeds are paid to your estate.

Naming a minor as benepisyaryo is allowed, but a legal guardian must be appointed to receive the proceeds until the child turns 18.

Riders

  • Accidental death benefit — doubles the sum assured if death results from an accident
  • Waiver of premium — future premiums waived if you become totally and permanently disabled
  • Critical illness — advances part of the sum assured on diagnosis of a listed condition
  • Hospital income — a daily amount for each day spent in hospital

Who can apply

Entry age is 18 to 65 for Term Life, 18 to 60 for Whole Life, and 21 to 55 for the Educational Plan. Applicants must be Philippine residents. Medical examination is required for sums assured above PHP 3,000,000, for applicants aged 51 and above, and where the health declaration indicates it.

Common questions

Magkano po ang premium ko?

It depends on your age, the plan, the sum assured and the payment term. As a guide, Term Life with a PHP 1,000,000 sum assured at age 30 starts at around PHP 780 monthly. Your agent can prepare an exact quote.

Ano po ang mangyayari kung ma-lapse ang policy ko?

Kung ma-lapse po, hihinto ang coverage. Pero kung may cash value na po ang policy niyo, pwedeng ituloy gamit ang automatic premium loan. Pwede rin pong i-reinstate sa loob ng 3 years.

Can I change my beneficiary later?

Yes, if the beneficiary is revocable. Submit a change of beneficiary form to any branch or through your agent. If the beneficiary is irrevocable, their written consent is required.

Is this the same as the bank's own product?

No. The Bank Partner Plan is underwritten by Solara and sold through the bank. The bank acts as a referral partner. Your policy is with Solara, and claims are handled by Solara.
