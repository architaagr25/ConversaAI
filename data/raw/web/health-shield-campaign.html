Why families choose Health Shield

Health Shield covers you when you are admitted to hospital, when you need day surgery, and when an accident means emergency treatment. Every plan level includes in-patient care, same-day procedures and urgent treatment at hospitals we work with.

Your annual limit runs to PHP 2,000,000 on our top plan, with room and board covered up to PHP 9,000 each day and intensive care up to PHP 25,000 each day. Out-patient consultations are included on Plus and Max, and everyone on those levels gets a yearly physical examination at no extra cost.

Cover that starts quickly

No long delays. Accidents are covered from day one, illnesses after just 30 days, and even pre-existing conditions are covered after only 30 days of membership. Apply today and you could be protected by the end of the month.

Simple to join

No medical examination needed. Apply online in minutes, answer a few short questions and get a decision fast. Add your spouse and children to the same plan and manage everything in one place.

Add the cover you need

  • Maternity cover for growing families
  • Treatment abroad in Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia
  • A lump sum if you are diagnosed with a critical illness
  • Cash for every day you spend in hospital
