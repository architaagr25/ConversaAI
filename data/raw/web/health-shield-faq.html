Health Shield: frequently asked questions

Do I need a medical examination to apply?

Most applicants do not. A medical questionnaire is required if you are 51 or older, or if you are applying for Max cover. A physical examination is arranged only when your answers to the questionnaire indicate one is needed. We will tell you before any examination is booked, and Solara pays for it.

When does my cover start?

Cover begins on the first day of the month after your application is approved and your first premium has been received. If your application is approved on 12 March and payment clears the same week, cover starts on 1 April.

How long do I have to wait before I can claim?

Accidents are covered immediately. Illnesses are covered after 30 days. If you have a condition that existed before you joined, it is covered once you have held the policy continuously for 2 years, as long as you told us about it when you applied.

What happens if I did not declare a condition?

An undeclared pre-existing condition is excluded permanently, and a claim relating to it will be declined. In serious cases the policy may be voided. If you are unsure whether something counts, declare it. Declaring a condition does not automatically mean higher premiums.

Can I add my family?

Yes. A spouse and children aged 6 months to 21 years may be added as dependants. Children over 21 need their own policy. Parents cannot be added as dependants but may apply separately if they are within the entry age limits.

What if I miss a payment?

There is a grace period of 31 days from the due date. Cover continues during the grace period. If payment has still not been received when the grace period ends, the policy lapses and claims are not payable. A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 6 months by paying the arrears, subject to a fresh health declaration.

Is dental included?

No. Dental treatment is not covered on any plan level. The only exception is reconstructive dental work needed because of an accident, which is treated as part of the accident claim.

Can I use any hospital?

Claims are settled directly at accredited hospitals, of which there are over 400. You may use a non-accredited hospital, but you will need to pay and then claim reimbursement, and reimbursement is capped at the accredited rate for the same treatment.

How do I make a claim?

For planned admissions, tell us at least 3 working days beforehand so we can issue a letter of guarantee. For emergencies, notify us within 24 hours of admission. Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 90 days of discharge with original receipts and the discharge summary.

Can I cancel?

You may cancel within 15 days of receiving your policy documents for a full refund, provided no claim has been made. After that, cancellation takes effect at the next renewal date and premiums already paid are not refunded.

Common concerns

"This is more expensive than I expected."

Cover can be adjusted. Moving from Plus to Essential lowers the monthly contribution considerably, and riders can be removed and added back at any renewal. Paying annually rather than monthly reduces the total by around 5 per cent. It is worth comparing what you would pay out of pocket for a single admission before deciding.

"I am already covered through my employer."

Employer cover usually ends when the employment does, and it rarely extends to dependants at the same level. A personal policy continues regardless of where you work, and the entry age you join at is the age your renewal terms are based on. Many members hold both.

"I would rather think about it."

That is reasonable. Two things are worth knowing while you decide: premiums are based on your age at entry, so waiting costs more later, and the pre-existing condition waiting period only starts once the policy does.

"I never claim, so it is wasted money."

Most members do not claim in a given year. The cover exists for the admission that would otherwise cost several hundred thousand pesos. If claim-free years matter to you, ask about the no-claim discount at renewal.
