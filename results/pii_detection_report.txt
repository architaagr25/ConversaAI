Known limits
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Name detection depends on a stop list built from this corpus, so
  it will not carry to a corpus it has not seen. It is off when
  rewriting knowledge base records and in the outbound guard, where
  a false positive would corrupt policy text or drop a live call.
  Addresses are matched to the locality, not the postcode, leaving
  about 2 per cent of a typical address string in place.
